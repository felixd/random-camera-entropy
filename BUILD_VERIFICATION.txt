Limitations of build environment
--------------------------------
- No physical camera, mTLS agent or the user's 200+ GB dataset was available.
- Parallel I/O speed depends on storage. NVMe commonly benefits from 4–16 hash
  workers; a single rotational disk may be faster with 1–2.
- The five final candidates each read the full dataset. More analysis workers
  increase CPU use but also multiply storage bandwidth demand.
